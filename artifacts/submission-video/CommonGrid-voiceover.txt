Saving electricity starts with small choices: switching off the lights, using air-conditioning carefully, and avoiding unnecessary power use. The result is hard to see, so motivation often fades.

CommonGrid makes every saving visible. Electricity saved becomes points, personal progress, and momentum towards a shared university reward. Students can immediately see their own impact, the university total, and exactly how close everyone is to the next milestone.

For this NUS prototype, students also represent their halls. Kent Ridge, Sheares, Eusoff, Temasek, and the other halls compete in an Energy Sprint. The hall that saves the most earns a stronger reward tier.

First place unlocks the Gold reward for the top forty percent of contributors. Second place unlocks Silver for the top thirty percent. Students still need to take part. A hall result alone does not guarantee a reward.

The Journey screen helps students build a habit. The calendar highlights every day electricity was saved. Streaks make consistency easy to understand, while achievements recognise milestones such as saving one hundred kilowatt-hours or becoming one of the hall's top contributors.

For this demo, an administrator simulates verified building data. In a full deployment, the same information could come from IoT-enabled electricity meters, so students would not need to record savings themselves.

Here, the administrator records eight kilowatt-hours for Alice. CommonGrid awards eighty points. The university moves from nine hundred and twenty to one thousand points, and three free laundry cycles are unlocked.

Kent Ridge finishes second and earns the Silver reward. Alice ranks twelfth out of eighty contributors, inside the top twenty-four, so she is eligible to redeem it. Students outside the cutoff keep their progress, while the reward goes to those who contributed most.

CommonGrid gives students immediate feedback, a reason to work together, and a clear connection between everyday choices and campus electricity use. Small savings become visible progress, friendly competition, and rewards people can genuinely earn.
